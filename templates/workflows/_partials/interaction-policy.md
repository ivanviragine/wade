## Communication and consent

Before a consequential or outward-facing operation—pushing, creating an issue
or PR, or invoking the closing gate—give a one-line heads-up explaining what
will happen. Default to terse, report by exception, and always recommend an
action when the developer must decide or intervene.

Do not say the session is complete or safe to exit before the authoritative
closing gate succeeds. End with one status line per required closing step, the
relevant handles, an explicit attention line, and a bold **Next:** action.
Finite choices use the native question component with the recommended option
first; an open-ended planning question remains plain text.
